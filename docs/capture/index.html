Onewheel handshake capture

For Floatface, a Garmin watch app that talks directly to your board.

What you'll need

  • A Makerdiary nRF52840-MDK USB Dongle running Nordic's nRF Sniffer for Bluetooth LE firmware. Using a different nRF52840 dongle or a Flipper Zero? The main README's manual instructions still work for you -- this tool is built around the Makerdiary dongle specifically.
  • Your phone, with the official Onewheel app installed and able to connect to your board.
  • Chrome, Edge, or Brave on a laptop or desktop. Not Firefox or Safari -- they don't support the Web Serial/File System Access APIs this tool depends on, and there's no workaround for that.

Nothing you do here leaves your computer. The dongle talks to your laptop over USB, everything is decoded locally in your browser, and the only output is a small text file you save yourself. No server, no account, no upload.

Flash your dongle

If your dongle is already running the nRF Sniffer firmware (for example, if you set it up using the manual README instructions previously), skip this step.

Otherwise: put the dongle in bootloader mode (usually a double-tap of its reset button -- check the dongle's own docs), which makes it show up as a USB drive named UF2BOOT. Then:

  1. Pick the .uf2 firmware file on your computer. Don't have one yet? Build or download Nordic's nRF Sniffer firmware per the main README's manual instructions, then come back here.
  2. Pick the UF2BOOT drive.

Capture the handshake

  1. Turn off your laptop's own Bluetooth. (Seriously -- this is the single biggest reason captures fail.)
  2. Power-cycle your board (off, then on). A fresh power-on seems to make the app re-run the full unlock handshake reliably.
  3. Connect to your dongle below, then wait for your board to appear.
  4. Tap your board's card, then open the official Onewheel app on your phone and let it connect. Keep the dongle within a few feet of your phone and board -- a weak signal is the second biggest reason captures fail.
